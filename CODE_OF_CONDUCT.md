# Community guidelines

Be welcoming, constructive and respectful of different backgrounds and experience levels.

- Discuss the work and ideas, without personal attacks.
- Give specific, helpful feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person’s private information.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, spam and sexualized content are not welcome.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. Apply these expectations consistently and explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

For abusive content, use GitHub’s reporting tools on the relevant comment or profile. For ordinary project concerns, contact the maintainer through an issue without including private details. Security vulnerabilities belong in the private channel described in SECURITY.md.
